# Sikai — learner demonstration

**Open it here: https://indragiri-ai.github.io/sikai-demo/**

A working demonstration of a bilingual (Nepali and English) mathematics learning
application for Nepal's basic level. This repository contains **only the compiled
demonstration**; the source lives in a separate private repository.

## What you can try

Pick a grade, then a lesson. Fifteen mathematics lessons across Grades 1, 2 and 3:
counting order, addition and subtraction within 20, two-digit sums, place value,
three- and four-digit arithmetic, division, long multiplication, fractions, and
comparing numbers.

Switch between **English and नेपाली** at any time. Open a hint before answering.
After each answer a worked explanation shows the method, in the chosen language.

It **works without internet**. Visit once, and it will open again with no
connection at all. On a phone you can add it to your home screen from the browser
menu and use it in airplane mode.

## Please read before judging it

This is an **engineering demonstration, not a finished product**.

- Every question, hint, explanation and translation is **original draft content
  that no teacher has reviewed**. Each lesson is marked "Awaiting teacher review"
  on screen, and that is accurate.
- The lesson topics and their grade placement follow the CDC basic level
  curriculum (आधारभूत तह कक्षा १–३, २०७६, परिमार्जन २०८२), but that alignment is
  this project's own reading of the document and has not been confirmed by a
  qualified reviewer.
- **Mathematics only.** No Nepali or English language content.
- **No gamification yet** — no games, points or badges.
- **No teacher accounts, student records or reports yet.** Answers are saved only
  in the browser on the device being used, and are not uploaded anywhere.
- Nothing you do here is recorded or sent to any server.

## What it does demonstrate

That the learning content, the bilingual interface, the offline behaviour and the
save-before-advance guarantee all work on a real device — including on a phone
with no signal.
